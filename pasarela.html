Tailoring: rules and exception

In the Women’s Fall/Winter 2026 Fashion Show, a return to origins unfolds, where the rigor of tailoring is revived through precise cuts, studied silhouettes and classic patterns such as wool pinstripes. The authority of established style codes is balanced by an exception to the rule itself, expressed through creative flair: the technical features of the double-breasted construction are echoed on the back of jackets.
